high-rate-spectrogram.webp
Frequency content of the DROPBEAR acceleration signal
Dataset by Austin Downey, Jonathan Hong, Jacob Dodson, Michael Carroll, and James Scheppegrell
https://github.com/High-Rate-SHM-Working-Group/Dataset-2-DROPBEAR-Acceleration-vs-Roller-Displacement/blob/main/data/DROPBEAR_dataset.json
CC BY-SA 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
New spectrogram of the source acceleration signal, sampled at 25.6 kHz, with an 8192-point Hann window, 7168-point overlap, and power spectral density (PSD) in dB relative to the largest PSD in the displayed frequency band. Display spans 0–800 Hz and clips the color scale at −55 dB. Adapted graphic licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
