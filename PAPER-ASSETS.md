The website includes verified PDF and DOI links where an exact match was found. Inline figures are linked only when a matching image was available; other records provide a direct link to figures inside the source PDF. The two missing PDFs have a Request PDF email link.

Six journal previews are loaded from the coauthor's public university website. They need an internet connection. The thin-wall manufacturing image is included locally and credited to the accompanying presentation.

To embed additional figures, add a PNG, JPG, or WebP from the corresponding paper to `assets/images/papers/`, fill in `figure`, `figure_caption`, and `figure_source` in `data/publications.json`, and run `python tools/build-publications.py`. Preserve any necessary figure attribution in the caption. See README.md for details.
