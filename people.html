Principal investigator

Emmanuel A.
Ogunniyi, Ph.D.

Department of Mechanical Engineering
San José State University

Emmanuel’s research connects intelligent manufacturing, innovative sensing, structural health monitoring, and real-time computational models. His work explores how physical measurements and digital models can help engineered systems respond to changing conditions.

Research themes include additive manufacturing monitoring, flexible capacitive sensors, digital twins, high-rate model updating, and sensing for structural batteries.
